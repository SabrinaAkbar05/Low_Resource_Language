01 The Problem Space
Empathy Map

What Our Users
Think, Feel & Do

Research uncovered a critical gap: Memoni is a living language with zero accessible digital learning pathways. Empathy mapping surfaced four deep patterns.

Think & Feel
  • → "My language is dying and nobody cares." — heritage guilt and cultural anxiety are pervasive among diaspora Gen Z.
  • → Embarrassed to speak Memoni incorrectly in front of elders; fear of being judged for mispronunciation.
  • → Anxious the language will disappear within one generation if nothing changes.
  • → Desire to connect with grandparents, but no bridge between English/Urdu and Memoni.
See
  • → Peers on Snapchat for 2+ hours daily — AR filters used for entertainment, never education.
  • → Absolutely zero apps, websites, or digital tools that teach Memoni at any level.
  • → Older relatives speaking a language they can't access or participate in meaningfully.
  • → Apps like Duolingo exist for major languages — nothing for Memoni or similar low-resource languages.
Say & Do
  • → "I wish there was a Duolingo for Memoni." — repeatedly surfaced across research conversations.
  • → Prefers micro-learning in 5-minute bursts — commute, queue, or idle moments.
  • → Motivated by streaks, social sharing, and visible progress — gamification is non-negotiable.
  • → Comfortable with AR filters in entertainment — learning via AR is a paradigm shift they're ready for.

HCI Design Principles — POUR

Every design decision is grounded in the WCAG 2.2 POUR accessibility framework.

P
Perceivable
Audio TTS + visual text. 5.2:1 contrast ratio. Real-time captions via Lens Studio's Caption feature.
O
Operable
Full-screen tap (44×44pt min). No precision targeting. Single-handed. No time pressure.
U
Understandable
Short imperative instructions. Dual script + phonetic display. Dot pagination. Pronunciation labels on all cards.
R
Robust
Standard Lens Studio APIs. Audio pre-loaded locally — works offline. Tested iOS + Android, 4-year-old devices.

07 Heuristic Evaluation
Nielsen's 10 Heuristics

Evaluated Against
Nielsen's 10 Heuristics

Meme_oni was assessed against all 10 of Nielsen's usability heuristics. Below is a summary of how each principle is addressed in the design.

H1 · Visibility of System Status
Always Know What's Happening
Immediate audio + card-swap feedback on tap. "Tap to Learn" hint on open. Dot-based pagination shows progress at all times — users always know where they are.
H2 · Match Between System and Real World
The "Heads Up" Metaphor
Flashcard placed on head mirrors the universally understood "Heads Up" party game. Vocabulary anchored in culturally familiar objects — Chai, Dates, family terms.
H3 · User Control and Freedom
Learner Controls the Pace
Cards do not auto-advance. Users stay on each card as long as needed and click only when ready. No time pressure, no "rushed by technology" feeling.
H4 · Consistency and Standards
Zero Learning Curve Across Levels
Identical interface across all three lenses — front camera, card on head, full-screen tap. Once learned, the interaction model transfers instantly to any new level.
H5 · Error Prevention
The Click-Anywhere Design
Full-screen tap eliminates the possibility of a "missed click." No small buttons. No precision required. Major accessibility win for low-vision users and users in motion.
H6 · Recognition Rather Than Recall
Everything Visible on Screen
English translation and Memoni Romanised text always visible simultaneously. Users recognise visual + audio prompts — no memorisation of previous cards required.
H7 · Flexibility and Efficiency of Use
One Tap — Maximum Efficiency
The single-tap system serves both cautious new learners and experienced users who cycle quickly through known words. No mode-switching, no multiple interaction patterns.
H8 · Aesthetic and Minimalist Design
Less Is More
Screen kept clear — user's face and flashcard only. HD paper-texture overlays without frames or borders. AR elements never overwhelm the live camera feed.
H9 · Help Recover from Errors
Continuous Loop — No Dead Ends
Accidentally skip a card? Click through the circular deck to return. No complex back-buttons or navigation dead ends. Recovery requires zero technical knowledge.
H10 · Help and Documentation
Just-In-Time Instruction
A single "Tap to Learn" prompt appears at the exact moment the lens opens. Because the interface is so simple, one instruction is sufficient — no manual or tutorial needed.

09 The Final Solution
Built in Lens Studio
Developed in Snapchat Lens Studio

Two AR Filters.
One Language Saved.

Meme_oni and Memonix are the first-ever interactive digital tools for learning Memoni. Both built in Snapchat Lens Studio — scan a Snapcode and start learning in seconds. No download. No account. No barrier.

Levels 1 & 2

Meme_oni

Vocabulary & Sentence Construction

👻
Level 1 — Vocabulary: Basic Memoni nouns — Morning, Lunch, Evening, Family, Food, Colours. AR overlay + TTS audio on tap.
Level 2 — Sentences: Subject-verb-object patterns, everyday greetings and phrases. Build real conversational Memoni.
Try Meme_oni on Snapchat
Level 3

Memonix

Counting & Numbers in AR

🔢
Level 3 — Counting: Cardinal numbers 1–100, ordinal usage. Count real-world objects through the live AR camera feed.
Culturally anchored: Chai cups, dates, and everyday Memoni objects serve as counting anchors — language meets lived reality.
